INDUSTRY UPDATE

Preparing for the new EU packaging rules

Logistics Insights Reading Time: 6 min

The landscape of European logistics is undergoing a tectonic shift with the introduction of Regulation (EU) 2025/40, also known as the Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation (PPWR). This landmark legislation aims to drastically reduce the environmental footprint of logistics operations by mandating stricter void-space ratios.

Compliance through Precision

Starting in 2025, shippers will be required to prove that their packaging configurations do not exceed specific void-space thresholds. For many, this means moving away from "one-size-fits-some" boxing toward high-precision automated calculation.

PackOpti provides the technical foundation for compliance by automatically documenting every shipment's volume optimization. Our system generates the necessary reporting data to verify that your operations meet the new environmental standards while simultaneously improving your bottom line.

"The shift from voluntary efficiency to regulatory mandate means that box optimization is no longer a 'nice-to-have'—it is a core operational requirement."

PackOpti supports packaging efficiency and reporting, but legal compliance should always be reviewed with regulatory experts.
